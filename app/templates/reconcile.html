What happens

  1. Both files are checked: running balance on every statement row, and opening + entries = closing for every branch in the bank book.
  2. Opening balances must agree with yesterday's closing and with the open items carried forward. A missing day or a back-dated ERP entry stops the run.
  3. Payments are matched one-to-one by amount and beneficiary. Collections are matched by UTR last‑4, then amount + name, then grouped.
  4. You get a BRS that must show a nil difference, a list of exceptions, and an Excel file.
