Bank holidays

Days the branches are closed, in addition to Sundays. Applies to every account.
On a holiday, money that reaches the bank is held for the next working day's bank book. If it isn't booked then, it is flagged Critical with the holiday date. Bank book entries dated on a holiday (or a Sunday) are flagged High as probably back-dated. A day with no bank book entries is treated as a holiday even if it isn't listed here. Adding it here also names it and enables the back-dating check.
